Mini Spotify Embed — Save a song

Paste a Spotify embed iframe (or a Spotify track URL). The player will appear to the right and saved songs will be stored locally (your browser).

Quick tips:

  • Paste Spotify embed iframe or public track URL.
  • Saved songs are kept in localStorage of this browser.
  • Click play to load the embedded player on the right.
